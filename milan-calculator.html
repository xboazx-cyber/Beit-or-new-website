Disclaimer: This calculator provides estimates only. Tax calculations depend on the cadastral value, property type, and individual tax circumstances. The registration tax for seconda casa is legally 9% of the cadastral value (rendita × 120) — if no rendita catastale is entered, the calculator uses 30% of the purchase price as an approximation of the cadastral value, which may not reflect your actual cadastral value.
