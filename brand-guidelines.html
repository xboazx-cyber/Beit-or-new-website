The Logo

Primary — On Cream
Reversed — On Dark
Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the letter ב around all edges of the logo at all times.

Minimum Size

Do not reproduce the logo smaller than 32px (digital) or 11mm (print) to preserve legibility of the circular detail.

Do Not Alter

Never stretch, rotate, recolor, or apply effects to the logo. Use only the approved colour versions provided.

Approved Backgrounds

Use the primary logo on cream or white backgrounds. Use the reversed version on dark brown or photographic backgrounds only.

Brand Voice

Beit Or Is
  • Warm and grounding
  • Poetic but never vague
  • Quietly confident
  • Rooted in nature and tradition
  • Inviting and unhurried
  • Honest and transparent
Beit Or Is Not
  • Clinical or cold
  • Trendy or hype-driven
  • Overly spiritual or abstract
  • Pushy or sales-heavy
  • Loud or attention-seeking
  • Generic "wellness" language
05

Design Principles

☀
Light

Let compositions breathe. White space is not emptiness — it is the pause that brings clarity.

⟡
Rooted

Draw from the earth. Textures, tones, and shapes should feel organic and hand-touched, never synthetic.

◌
Whole

Every element serves the whole. Restraint is a creative act — add only what belongs.

06

Imagery Style

Warm Natural Light

Golden hour tones, soft shadows, no harsh flash or artificial lighting.

Living Nature

Herbs, leaves, earth, water. Always real, never artificial or over-produced.

Human Presence

Candid, unhurried moments. People at rest, in practice, in quiet connection.

Photography should feel like something you remember, not something you've seen before. Avoid stock imagery clichés. Favour imperfection, texture, and genuine presence over polish.
